# Lab 2 — Agile Backlog Creation & Sprint Simulation in Jira

**Project:** Pharmacy Expiry & Re-order Dispatch Engine  
**Student SRN:** PES1UG24CS553

## Objective
Convert the Lab 1 pharmacy-system requirements into Jira Epics and User Stories, prioritize and estimate the backlog, simulate two Scrum sprints, and inspect sprint progress using Jira's Burndown Chart.

## Suggested Jira setup
- Project name: **Pharmacy Expiry and Reorder System**
- Project template: **Software Development → Scrum**
- Suggested Epics:
  1. Inventory and Batch Management
  2. Expiry and FEFO Management
  3. Automated Re-order Management

## Backlog
`Backlog.csv` contains suggested Epics' related User Stories, priorities, story points, and sprint allocation. Create the Epics in Jira first, then create the stories and link each story to the correct Epic. Estimates are suggested starting values; discuss and adjust them with your team using Planning Poker.

## Sprint plan
- **Sprint 1:** ST-01, ST-02, ST-03 — 13 story points. Focus: core inventory and expiry visibility.
- **Sprint 2:** ST-04, ST-05, ST-06, ST-07 — 19 story points. Focus: FEFO, low-stock detection, and replenishment workflow.

Use the dates and duration required by your instructor. For the handout simulation, move selected stories through **To Do → In Progress → Done**, then complete each sprint. Do not mark work Done until you have actually moved the corresponding Jira items.
